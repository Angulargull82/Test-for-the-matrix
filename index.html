Quick guide to the controls, shortcuts, and moving parts.

Enter on the home screen opens the login flow if needed, or the menu if you are already logged in.
G toggles glitch mode.
R toggles rain on or off.
S toggles sound.
F toggles fullscreen.
H opens the menu.
